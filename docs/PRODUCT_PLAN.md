# Product Plan: QR Doorbell Platform

## 1) Product vision
Build a communication platform that replaces the physical doorbell using QR codes and browser-based messaging.

## 2) Target users
- **Primary**: apartment residents, homeowners, hosts, small offices.
- **Secondary**: property managers, gated communities, student housing.

## 3) Value proposition
- No hardware wiring required.
- Visitors need no app and no account.
- Residents can respond from anywhere by text/video/voice.
- Full conversation history and missed-visitor records.

## 4) Pricing model ideas
- **Free tier**: text + missed messages, limited history.
- **Pro individual** ($4–$8/month): video, voice archive, anti-spam, custom greeting.
- **Building plan**: bulk resident management + concierge routing + analytics.

## 5) Core feature set
### Essential (MVP)
1. Resident account and identity verification.
2. Unique resident URL + QR generation.
3. Visitor page (chat/video/voice options).
4. Real-time resident notifications.
5. Conversation and message history.

### Expansion features
1. AI smart replies and translation.
2. Delivery mode (“drop package and capture photo”).
3. Time-based routing (do-not-disturb, office hours).
4. Multi-user household accounts.
5. Building concierge handoff.

## 6) Legal/compliance considerations
- Consent notice before recording audio/video.
- Data retention controls (auto-delete options).
- GDPR/CCPA privacy options and export/delete tooling.
- Abuse controls and visitor rate-limiting.

## 7) Go-to-market strategy
- Start with urban apartments and host communities.
- Partner with property managers for B2B pilot.
- Provide printable “smart door card” kits.
- Referral incentives: invite neighbors for discounts.
